# BubbleApp v2

BubbleApp started as my apprenticeship project, a point-of-sale app for ordering bubble waffles and trying out Solana payments. With the project restored and running on .NET 11, I'm planning a second version built around a simpler counter workflow.

- **A new design for phones and tablets.** Bring back the product photos and Waffle Moments branding from the original mockups. On tablets, the menu and current order can sit side by side. On phones, a compact menu and a bottom order bar keep the total and checkout within easy reach.
- **A smoother ordering workflow.** Make it quick to add items, adjust quantities, add a custom Moment, and review the order before choosing how to pay.
- **Card and crypto payments.** Keep menu prices and order totals in euros. At checkout, offer in-person card payments and let customers pay with a choice of supported cryptocurrencies.
- **An Admin tab for the menu.** Let authorized users add and edit items, change prices, and make items available or unavailable from within the app. Price changes apply to future orders so completed orders retain what the customer paid.

This is the plan for v2. The first milestone is the redesigned order screen, followed by menu management and payment integration. Payment providers and the exact list of supported cryptocurrencies will be decided during testing.
